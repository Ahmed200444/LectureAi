How it works

Record the real lecture first. AI comes after.

LectureAI is built around preserving the original recording as the source of truth. The browser recording path does not treat microphone permission alone as success: it verifies that the microphone stream can produce encoded, non-silent audio before a lecture starts.

1. Verify
Record and replay a short microphone sample before class.
2. Record
Capture encoded audio in frequent local checkpoints with no LectureAI-imposed minute limit.
3. Preserve
Assemble and reload the finished audio before calling the recording saved.
4. Transcribe
Use multilingual Whisper locally on supported devices, or the optional local Windows helper.

Does LectureAI work on iPhone and iPad?

Safari and an installed Add to Home Screen PWA are supported targets. The browser version preserves frequent checkpoints and warns about background/suspension risk, but iOS and iPadOS do not guarantee indefinite browser microphone recording while an app is backgrounded or the device is locked.

Does LectureAI upload recordings to the cloud?

Not by default. Recordings and lecture data are stored locally. Speech-model files may need to be downloaded, but local transcription does not require sending the lecture recording to a paid speech service.

What languages can it transcribe?

LectureAI uses multilingual Whisper models with automatic language detection rather than an English-only model. Accuracy varies by language, accent, audio quality, distance, background noise, and model size, so the original recording remains the authority for corrections.

Are there recording or transcription quotas?

LectureAI does not impose recording-minute or transcription-minute quotas. Real device limits still exist, including storage capacity, memory pressure, battery, browser suspension, and model-download size.
