Run the unified LectureAI app

Install Expo Go

How the free testing flow works

1Install Expo Go from the App Store on your iPhone and iPad.

2On the computer that has the LectureAI repository, open the expo-recorder folder and start the Expo development server. Expo displays a QR code.

3Scan that QR code with the iPhone or iPad and open the project in Expo Go. The screen you see is the unified LectureAI app, not the old one-file Lecture Recorder Snack.

4Use LectureAI to record, pause/continue, mark moments, preserve and replay the original audio, manage lectures, transcribe through the paired Windows helper, review/edit the transcript, generate source-grounded notes/study material, and export.

What Cloudflare does

Cloudflare Pages can host the LectureAI website, SEO pages, privacy/how-it-works pages, and these installation/testing instructions. It does not convert the Expo project into a downloadable iPhone or iPad app.

During this free testing stage, Expo Go is the app container. A future standalone LectureAI icon/app requires Apple's normal signed-app distribution path.

Important recording limitation

Keep Expo Go open during important lectures. Stock Expo Go cannot apply LectureAI-specific background-audio entitlement/configuration, so locked-screen or long background recording is not guaranteed.

That is why the physical acceptance gate tests both iPhone and iPad separately before this branch is merged.

Export from either Apple device

Inside a lecture, use the export controls to share or Save to Files. Original audio remains separate and unchanged. Transcript exports use your corrected text; notes/study exports are blocked if they are stale after a transcript edit until you regenerate them.
