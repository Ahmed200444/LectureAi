Privacy

Your lecture content stays local by default.

LectureAI is designed so the original recording, transcript, notes, course information, bookmarks, and imported lecture files are stored on the device running the app unless you explicitly export or share them.

What stays on your device

  • Original lecture recordings and recording checkpoints.
  • Transcripts, corrections, translations, notes, bookmarks, and course metadata.
  • Generated study material created from the local transcript.

What network access can occur

The web app itself and software assets are downloaded from the LectureAI site. On-device transcription can also download speech-model files from the model host when the model is not already cached. Those model downloads do not require uploading your lecture audio.

On Windows, LectureAI can use an optional helper that listens only on the local computer's loopback address. The hosted browser interface sends a recording to that local helper only when you choose the computer transcription action.

No mandatory paid transcription provider

LectureAI does not require a paid speech API, subscription, recording-minute quota, or transcription-minute quota. Real limits can still come from the browser, device storage, battery, memory, and operating-system behavior.

Search engines do not protect private lecture data

Lecture content is not published as public web pages. LectureAI does not rely on robots.txt as a privacy boundary for recordings, transcripts, or notes; those items remain in local application storage instead of becoming crawlable URLs.

Open LectureAI
LectureAI is local-first software. Device and browser limitations still apply, especially for long browser recordings and background behavior on iPhone and iPad.
