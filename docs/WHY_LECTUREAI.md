# Why LectureAI

LectureAI focuses on a practical problem in student workflows: capturing lectures reliably enough that later notes or AI-generated text can be checked against the original source.

The project is local-first by default and treats the original recording as the source of truth. It emphasizes visible microphone readiness, device diagnostics, local playback, and transparent limitations rather than claiming perfect transcription or background recording behavior that has not been measured.

The long-term direction is to combine reliable capture with multilingual lecture transcription and timestamp-linked review while keeping evaluation reproducible and privacy-conscious.
